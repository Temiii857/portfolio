One Piece SBS Analysis Project

This project explored character relationships and storytelling patterns within the One Piece SBS volumes through network visualization and data analysis. I organized and analyzed dialogue data to better understand recurring connections, themes, and interactions between characters across the series.

The final result was an interactive visualization project that combined digital humanities research, structured data, and visual storytelling.

Jamble – UI/UX & Product Design Concept

Jamble is a UI/UX and product design concept focused on creating a safer, more age-appropriate online space for preteens. I designed interface mockups that emphasized accessibility, emotional comfort, simple navigation, and creative interaction.

This project applied human factors and user-centered design principles to explore how digital products can better support younger users through safer communication and intuitive design.

The final result included polished UI screens, branding concepts, and a research infographic explaining the design process and usability goals.

The Space Between Us (Twine)

The Space Between Us is an interactive story game created using Twine. I designed branching story paths, dialogue interactions, and multiple endings focused on friendship, grief, and emotional decision-making.

This project explored storytelling, interaction design, and player experience through choice-based gameplay.

The final result was a playable narrative experience where player decisions directly influenced the story outcome.

Spider-Man Markup Project

This project focused on building a structured HTML and CSS website based on The Amazing Spider-Man comic series. I worked with semantic markup, accessibility practices, and responsive web design principles.

The final result was a visually organized and accessible website that demonstrated front-end development and layout design skills.

Thinglink Project

For this collaborative project, my team created and annotated a 3D model of a space in Erie Hall using Thinglink. The project focused on spatial interaction, digital annotation, and interactive exploration.

The final result was an immersive 3D environment that allowed users to explore and interact with information directly within the modeled space.

Corpus Analysis Project

This project compared SZA’s Supermodel and Sailorr’s Pookie’s Requiem through corpus and text analysis methods. I analyzed repeated language, emotional tone, and thematic patterns across both songs.

The final result included visual analysis graphics and written interpretation exploring how artists communicate heartbreak, rejection, and self-reflection through language.

Reflection

Through this analysis, I explored how artists communicate emotions differently through repeated language, tone, and themes.

Dear Data Project

Dear Data was a hand-drawn data visualization project tracking how often I checked the time throughout one week. I collected personal behavioral data and transformed it into a visual storytelling experience.

The final result combined observation, organization, and visual communication to present everyday habits through design.
