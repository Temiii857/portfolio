Jamble – UI/UX & Product Design Concept

Jamble is a UI/UX concept project focused on creating a safer and more age-appropriate online space for preteens. The design uses soft pastel colors, rounded components, and simple navigation to create a welcoming and user-friendly experience.

This project also explores human factors principles by focusing on accessibility, emotional comfort, ease of navigation, and safer online interaction for younger users.
