Corpus Analysis: SZA’s "Supermodel" and Sailorr’s "Pookie’s Requiem"

I chose SZA’s "Supermodel" and Sailorr’s "Pookie’s Requiem" because they both explore themes of heartbreak, self-reflection, and dealing with rejection, but they approach these themes in different ways. I’m familiar with SZA’s style but had less experience with Sailorr, so I wanted to see how their lyrics compare and contrast. My goal was to find the differences in how each artist portrays emotions of longing and loss.

You

One of the most common words in both songs was “you.” In "Supermodel", SZA uses “you” to directly address her ex, expressing her frustrations and how his actions affected her self-esteem. In "Pookie’s Requiem", Sailorr uses “you” in a more detached way, reflecting on how their ex has moved on and what that means for them. Both songs show how much importance the other person holds, but SZA’s is more confrontational while Sailorr’s feels sad and accepting.

Dream

Another interesting word was “dream,” which is repeated several times in "Pookie’s Requiem" but not in "Supermodel". Sailorr uses “dream” to symbolize what could have been in an alternate reality, imagining a version of the relationship where things went differently. In contrast, SZA focuses more on the reality of what happened and how it impacted her self-image. The use of “dream” highlights the way Sailorr holds onto the past while SZA seems to be working through it and moving forward.

Reflection

After comparing these two songs, I noticed how both artists write about rejection and heartbreak but in unique ways. SZA’s "Supermodel" feels raw and bold, showing both vulnerability and anger, while Sailorr’s "Pookie’s Requiem" is more about mourning and letting go. Both approaches show different sides of heartbreak, and this comparison gave me a deeper appreciation for how lyrics can reflect personal experiences in different tones. If I could compare further, I would include another SZA song like "20 Something", which also deals with nostalgia and loss, to expand on these themes.
